Kentaro Kobayashi (小林 賢太朗)

About

I am a Ph.D. student at Programming Logic Lab., University of Tsukuba, supervised by Yukiyoshi Kameyama. My research interests are in theoretical aspects of computation and programming languages. Specifically, I have been interested in the theory of control operators and continuations—their type systems, logics, and semantics.

I am also a research assistant in the Continuum Computing Trustworthiness Research Group at AIST (National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology), where I work on formal verification of statistical programs.
