Kentaro Kobayashi

Kentaro Kobayashi (小林 賢太朗)

I am a Ph.D. student at Programming Logic Lab., University of Tsukuba, supervised by Yukiyoshi Kameyama. My research interests are in programming language theory; specifically, I have been interested in the theory of control operators and continuations---their type systems, logics, and semantics.

I am also working as a research assistant at AIST (National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology) and working on the formalization and verification of statistical programs.
